How this works

Each job type has a cost per square foot, or a flat range for work that doesn't scale with house size, drawn from the ranges published in our guides. We then adjust for whether the job is straightforward, and for labour and overhead differences across the corridor — Austin and the northern suburbs run highest, San Antonio and the outlying towns lowest.

This is a planning tool, not a quote. Its purpose is to tell you whether a contractor's number is in the right territory, and to give you a figure to compare three quotes against.

The single best thing you can do: get three written quotes on anything over $2,000, and ask every contractor the same questions so you compare like for like. Our guides list the exact questions to ask for each job.
