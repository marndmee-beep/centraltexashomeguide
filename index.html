What home repairs actually cost in Central Texas

Local prices for roofs, air conditioning, water heaters, foundations and plumbing across Austin, Round Rock, Cedar Park, Georgetown, San Marcos, New Braunfels and San Antonio.

Why prices here are different

Central Texas is hard on houses in ways that other parts of the country are not, and that shows up in what you pay:

  • The heat. Air conditioners here run eight or nine months a year, so systems reach the end of their life around 12 to 15 years instead of the 20 quoted nationally.
  • The sun. UV damages asphalt shingles faster than in cooler states. A roof expected to last 25 years in Ohio may give you 15 to 18 here.
  • The clay soil. Expansive clay swells when it rains and shrinks in drought. That movement is behind most foundation problems in the region.
  • Hail. The I-35 corridor sits in one of the most hail-prone parts of the country, which is why roof claims are so common.
  • Limestone and hard water. Water heaters fill with scale, which shortens their life and raises energy bills.

Typical costs in the region

These are ranges for standard homes in the Austin to San Antonio corridor. Your quote will depend on the size of the house, the material you choose and how accessible the work is.

Get more than one quote. Local prices for the same job vary widely between companies, and the difference is often not quality but how busy a company is and how they price overhead. Three quotes on any job over $2,000 is standard practice.
