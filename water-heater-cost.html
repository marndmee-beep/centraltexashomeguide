Water heater replacement cost in Central Texas

Tank prices, tankless prices, and the reason water heaters in this region don't last as long as the label claims.

The short answer

A standard 40 to 50 gallon tank water heater, installed, runs $1,200 to $2,600 in the Austin and San Antonio area. A tankless system runs $3,000 to $6,500 installed, more if it needs a gas line upgrade or a larger vent.

Why they don't last as long here

Water in much of Central Texas is hard, carrying dissolved limestone. When that water is heated, the minerals come out of solution and settle as scale on the inside of the tank and on the heating elements. A tank with an inch of scale heats more slowly, works harder, and the steel beneath it corrodes faster.

Practically, that means a tank quoted as a 12-year unit may give you nine. Flushing the tank once a year removes some of that scale and is the single cheapest thing you can do to extend its life. Most homeowners never do it.

Signs it's failing: rumbling or popping noises when it heats, rusty water at the hot tap, water pooling at the base, or hot water running out faster than it used to. Any of these means start budgeting — and a leaking tank can cause thousands in water damage if it's in the attic or an interior closet.

Tank or tankless?

  • Tankless costs two to three times more up front and needs annual descaling here, because of the water. Budget $150 to $300 a year for that, or do it yourself with a kit.
  • It lasts roughly twice as long, which is the main financial argument. Over 20 years the two options come closer than the sticker price suggests.
  • Unlimited hot water matters if you have a large household or a big tub. If you live alone, it rarely pays back.
  • Installation is where surprises live. Tankless units need adequate gas supply and venting. In older homes that can add $1,000 or more to the job.

Questions to ask the installer

  • Does the price include a new expansion tank, pan and drain line, or is that extra?
  • Will you pull the permit? A water heater replacement requires one in most Central Texas jurisdictions.
  • What's the warranty on parts, and separately on labour?
  • Is the old unit hauled away?
