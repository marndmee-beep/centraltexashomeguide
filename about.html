About this site

Central Texas Home Guide publishes plain-spoken cost information for homeowners between Austin and San Antonio.

Why it exists

When your air conditioning dies in July, you have a day to make a decision that costs thousands, and the information available is mostly written by the companies selling the work. This site exists to give the other half of the picture: what a job should cost, what questions to ask, and where the common traps are.

How the numbers are researched

  • Published rate cards and price lists from national and regional companies.
  • Contractor quotes and invoices from homeowners in the corridor, shared with permission.
  • Material costs from suppliers, tracked over time.
  • Industry cost surveys and trade association data, adjusted for this region.

Prices change. Ranges here are a starting point for a conversation, not a bid. Any figure on this site should be checked against live local quotes before you commit money.

How the site is funded

Two ways: display advertising, and affiliate commissions when a reader signs up for a tool or service through our links. Affiliate links are always disclosed on the page they appear on. Commissions never change a recommendation — where we have a view, we say it plainly, including when the answer is "don't buy this".

What this site is not

  • Not an insurance, legal or financial adviser. Policy and contract questions belong with a licensed professional.
  • Not a contractor directory, and we don't sell leads to individual businesses.
  • Not a substitute for a proper inspection by a qualified tradesperson.

Corrections

If a figure here is wrong for your area, tell us and we'll look into it. Local accuracy is the whole point of the site.
