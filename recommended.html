For tradespeople and contractors

Quoting and invoicing software

If you are still writing quotes by hand or in a spreadsheet, a quoting tool pays for itself the first week. Look for one that lets you quote from your phone on site, itemises materials and labour separately, and tracks whether a quote was viewed.

Liability insurance for trades

Most commercial clients and general contractors will not let you on site without a certificate of insurance. If you are quoting bigger jobs, get cover sized to the work you take on, and keep a PDF certificate on your phone.

For homeowners

Water leak sensors

Cheap insurance against the most expensive common failure in a Texas home. A leaking water heater or supply line in an attic can cause five figures of damage. A sensor that alerts your phone costs less than a call-out fee.

Read about slab leaks and what they cost →

Home warranty and emergency cover

Worth it for some, not for others. If your systems are old, a warranty can smooth out the year; if your house is new, it usually is not worth the premium. Read the exclusions before buying.

AC maintenance plans

An annual service visit keeps a new system inside its warranty and catches a failing capacitor before it fails in July. Ask any company you are considering what the plan includes and what it excludes.

How we choose

We list a product only if we would recommend it to a neighbour, and we explain the reasoning so you can judge it for yourself. Where we have a view, we say it plainly, including when the answer is that something is not worth the money.

If a recommendation stops being worth making, we remove it.
