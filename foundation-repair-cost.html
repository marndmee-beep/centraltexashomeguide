Foundation repair cost in Central Texas

Why the clay under your house moves, what repair actually costs, and how to tell a real problem from a cosmetic crack.

The short answer

Foundation repair in the Austin and San Antonio area runs $4,000 to $25,000, with most single-family jobs landing between $6,000 and $12,000. Steel pier installations typically cost $1,000 to $1,500 per pier, and a typical home needs eight to 20 of them.

Why this region has so much of it

The soil under much of Central Texas is expansive clay. When it rains, the clay absorbs water and swells. In a dry summer, it shrinks. Your foundation sits on top of that movement, and over a decade or two the house flexes.

Drought years are the pressure point. During the 2011 and 2022 droughts, foundation companies across the corridor were booked out for months. After a wet spring following a dry summer, calls spike again.

The cheapest fix is water management. Keeping the soil around your foundation at a consistent moisture level prevents most of this. Soaker hoses on a timer in summer, gutters that discharge well away from the slab, and grading that slopes away from the house do more than any repair you can buy.

Signs worth investigating

  • Doors and windows that stick in one season and free up in another.
  • Diagonal cracks in interior walls, especially near door frames.
  • A floor that slopes enough to notice, or tiles that crack in a line.
  • Gaps opening between the wall and the ceiling or floor trim.
  • Bricks with stair-step cracking in the mortar.

Hairline cracks in drywall are normal and usually cosmetic. What matters is whether the movement is active. Your first step is not a repair quote — it's a foundation inspection with elevation measurements, so you know how much the house has actually moved.

How to buy this job well

  • Ask for elevations, not opinions. A real inspection measures the floor across the house and shows you the numbers.
  • Get the repair engineered or inspected. Ask whether the design is from a licensed professional engineer. Many lenders and buyers will want that paperwork later.
  • Beware the lifetime warranty that requires annual paid inspections you must never miss. Read what voids it.
  • Fix drainage first. If water pools at one corner, repair without drainage work will not hold.
  • Get two or three quotes. Pier counts for the same house vary significantly between companies.
